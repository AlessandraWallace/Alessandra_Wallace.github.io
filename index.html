PROJECT 01 · POLICY RESEARCH

New Jersey Substance-Use Treatment Policy Memo

Focus: Public policy, healthcare systems, and program accountability.

Developed a policy proposal addressing how New Jersey can strengthen the evaluation of state-funded substance-use treatment centers. The memo explored moving beyond payment models that focus mainly on services delivered and toward a system that also tracks patient outcomes.

The proposed approach centered on shared performance measures—such as treatment completion, patient retention, and post-discharge stability—and a centralized data-sharing system. The project required reviewing research and policy sources, identifying implementation considerations, and translating evidence into a concise recommendation for a policy audience.

Skills demonstrated: Policy research, source evaluation, critical thinking, data-informed analysis, and professional writing.

PROJECT 02 · BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Late-Night Campus Coffee Cart Concept

Focus: Business strategy, customer convenience, and team collaboration.

Worked with a team to develop a concept for portable coffee carts located in Rutgers libraries and academic buildings during late-night study hours. The idea focused on making coffee and snacks more accessible to students without requiring them to leave their study spaces.

The concept included accepting meal swipes as a payment option and considered how the business could expand into the Canadian market. The team examined the customer experience, the barista role, and practical elements of operating a small mobile service. I also contributed to the final presentation by editing the team video and improving its audio.

Skills demonstrated: Teamwork, business planning, customer-focused thinking, presentation development, and multimedia editing.

PROJECT 03 · AI & MARKETING

AI-Assisted Influencer Marketing Analysis

Focus: Digital marketing, AI-supported workflows, and campaign evaluation.

Analyzed how a fitness brand offering an app with cryptocurrency-based rewards could use influencer marketing to reach and engage its audience. The project considered how to select influencers based on audience relevance, credibility, engagement, and alignment with the brand.

It also examined how AI can help draft promotional messages and measure campaign performance, while recognizing the need for human review. In particular, the analysis considered how influencers’ personal experiences can make content feel more authentic and why marketers should review AI-generated messages and performance data before making decisions.

Skills demonstrated: Marketing strategy, AI literacy, audience analysis, campaign measurement, and critical evaluation.
